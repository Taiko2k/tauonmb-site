## Other builds

- **Portable Linux builds** for [x86-64](https://github.com/Taiko2k/Tauon/releases/latest/download/TauonMusicBox-linux.7z) and [ARM64](https://github.com/Taiko2k/Tauon/releases/latest/download/TauonMusicBox-linux-arm64.7z), packed as .7z.
- **AppImage**: an unofficial build maintained by the community at [pkgforge-dev/Tauon-AppImage](https://github.com/pkgforge-dev/Tauon-AppImage).
- **Older versions** are on [GitHub releases](https://github.com/Taiko2k/Tauon/releases).

## After installing

:::tip[Flatpak and music folders]
The Flatpak runs in a sandbox. If your music is outside your home folder, for example in `/mnt` or on another drive, give Tauon access to it with [Flatseal](https://flathub.org/apps/com.github.tchx84.Flatseal).
:::

Then see [First launch](/manual/overview/#first-launch) to import your music.
