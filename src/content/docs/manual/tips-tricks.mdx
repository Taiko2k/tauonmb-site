## Hidden Shift options

Several menus have extra items that only appear while you hold `Shift`, including **↳ After Current Track** (queue a track next), **Delete Track File**, **Lock** for playlists, and **Vacuum Playtimes**. Try holding `Shift` while right-clicking.

## Make a random playlist of 10 tracks

Right-click a playlist tab and choose **Generate… › Shuffled Tracks**. Then right-click the new playlist, choose **Edit Generator…**, and add ` n10` to the end of the code. Choose **Regenerate** whenever you want a fresh set.

## Build playlists from search

In [global search](/manual/search/), hold `Ctrl` while clicking results to add them to the playlist you're viewing. Search stays open, so you can quickly collect several artists or albums.

Folder names show up in search results too. Right-click any result to find it in its playlist without playing it.

## Transfer play counts to another track

To move play counts, loves and ratings from one track to another, for example to a better-quality copy:

1. Select the old track and press `Ctrl` + `C`.
2. Select the new track and press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `P`.

Press `Ctrl` + `Z` to undo.

## Make a playlist from the queue or play history

- Right-click the [queue](/manual/queue/) and choose **Queue to New Playlist**.
- Choose **Database… › Play History to Playlist** from the main menu to list what you've played recently.

## Radio Random

Press `/` (or choose **Radio Random** from the playback menu) to jump to a random track at a random point, like flicking through radio stations.

## Revert

Skipped a track by mistake? Press `Shift` + `/` (or **Revert** in the playback menu) to go back to it, from where you left off.

## Shuffle Lockdown

Right-click the shuffle button and choose **Shuffle Lockdown** for a "party mode": the playlist plays in shuffle and the controls are hidden. Press `Esc` to leave.

## Widen the side panel past square

Tap `Shift` while dragging the side panel's edge to make it wider than the album art. Useful for wide or rectangular artwork.

## Time cursor

Middle-click the seek bar to show a cursor with the time at that point.

## Slow down or speed up

Use the speed slider in the playback menu (☰) to change playback speed from 0.1× to 3×. Handy for practising along to a song.

## Copy the playing track's title

Right-click empty space in the bottom panel and choose **Copy Title to Clipboard**.

## Physically delete a folder

Right-click a track and choose **Meta… › Modify Folder…**. The box has a **Trash** button. Hold `Shift` to change it to **Delete**. See [Folder Management](/manual/folder-management/).

## Fix garbled Japanese text (mojibake)

If Japanese text in your tags displays as random accented characters, it was probably saved in the wrong encoding. The best fix is to correct the tags:

1. Select the album's tracks and choose **Edit Tags…**.
2. In the [tag editor](/manual/tag-editor/), choose **Tools › Fix Mojibake**.
3. Check the result, then click **Write tags**.

For a quick fix that only changes Tauon's database, not your files, right-click a track and choose **Meta… › Fix Mojibake**. To undo it, use **Meta… › Rescan Tags**.
