---
title: Discord
description: Discord Rich Presence integration
---

Discord Rich Presence shows what you're listening to on your Discord profile.

To turn it on, go to **Settings › Connect › Sharing** and turn on **Enable Discord Rich Presence**. The status line underneath shows whether Tauon is connected to Discord.

Once it's on, you can choose:

- **Discord card order**: whether the song or the artist comes first
- **Member list shows**: whether the member list shows the song or the artist
- **Clean title (Removes .feat etc)**
- **Last.fm link button** and **Tauon website button** on your profile card
- **Keep idle**: keep showing the presence while paused or stopped
- **Upload to catbox.moe**: shows your own album art on Discord. A small copy of the cover is uploaded to litterbox.catbox.moe, where it's deleted after 72 hours. Without this, Tauon looks up the cover on MusicBrainz.

:::note[Arch Linux]
Install the AUR dependency `python-pypresence`.
:::
