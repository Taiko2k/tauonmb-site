The **Artist List** mode of the left panel lists the artists in the playlist you're viewing.

<Image src={artistListImg} alt="The artist list in the left panel" width={410} densities={[1, 2]} />

To show it, right-click the side-panel button (the third button in the top-left corner) and choose **Artist List**.

## How it works

- **Click** an artist to jump to their first album in the playlist. **Double-click** to play.
- With the mouse over the list, type a letter to jump to artists starting with it.
- **Go to playing** (`'`) also highlights the playing artist in the list.
- Right-click an artist and choose **Filter to New Playlist** to make a playlist of just that artist.

    :::tip
    The new playlist stays linked to the artist list. Clicking **Filter to New Playlist** on another artist replaces the contents rather than creating yet another playlist.
    :::

- Right-click › **View…** to sort the list alphabetically, by popularity or by playtime, to hide thumbnails, or to hide artists with only a few tracks.

## Artist thumbnails

Artist pictures are downloaded from last.fm and fanart.tv. If no picture appears, a matching artist probably wasn't found. Tauon won't retry those artists until you choose **Database… › Reset Image Cache** from the main menu.

You can also add your own pictures. Put them in the `artist-pictures` folder inside Tauon's [data folder](/manual/app-data/), named exactly after the artist, for example `Coldplay.jpg` or `The Killers.png`.

:::caution
**Database… › Clear Artist Cache** deletes every picture in the `artist-pictures` folder, including ones you added yourself. Keep a copy of your own pictures somewhere else.
:::
