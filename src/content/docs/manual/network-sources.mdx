As well as local files, Tauon can play music from:

- [**Jellyfin**](/manual/jellyfin/)
- [**PLEX**](https://www.plex.tv/)
- **Subsonic**-compatible servers such as [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/), [Airsonic](https://airsonic.github.io/) and [Nextcloud Music](https://github.com/owncloud/music)
- **TIDAL**
- Another copy of **Tauon** on your network

Each has a tile in **Settings › Connect**. Click a tile to open its settings.

## PLEX and Subsonic servers

<Steps>
1. Make sure your server is set up, running and reachable from your computer.
2. In Tauon, go to **Settings › Connect** and click the **PLEX** or **Airsonic** tile. (Airsonic covers all Subsonic-compatible servers.)
3. Fill in your account details. For PLEX, also enter the server and library names.
4. Click **Import music to playlist**.
</Steps>

The imported playlist ("PLEX Collection" or "Airsonic Collection") holds your server's library. Keep this playlist, even if you make other playlists from it.

:::note
Imported network tracks don't update automatically. To refresh, right-click the playlist tab and choose **Regenerate**.

Server-side playlists aren't imported.
:::

Server options:

- **Use plain text authentication** (Subsonic): needed for Nextcloud Music.
- **Allow local scrobbling**: turn off if your server already [scrobbles](/manual/scrobbling/) your plays, to avoid double scrobbles.

:::caution[Limitations]
- Only one server of each type is supported.
- If you switch or reset a server, delete any playlists with tracks from it. To remove all network tracks, choose **Database… › Remove Network Tracks** from the main menu.
:::

## TIDAL

<Steps>
1. Click the **TIDAL** tile, then **Login**. Your web browser opens.
2. Sign in to TIDAL. When the browser lands on a page that doesn't load, copy that page's full address.
3. Back in Tauon, click **Paste redirect URL**.
</Steps>

Then use **Import albums** and **Import tracks** to import your TIDAL favourites. You can also paste a TIDAL album, playlist, mix, track or artist link into a playlist with `Ctrl` + `V`.

:::note
TIDAL support needs the optional `tidalapi` Python package.
:::

## Network cache

Settings for streaming are in **Settings › Audio › Cache**:

- **Use persistent network cache** keeps downloaded tracks between sessions, up to the **Cache size** you set.
- **Wait for entire file** downloads each track fully before playing, instead of streaming it.

### Offline copies

You can keep compressed copies of network tracks for offline use. [Transcode](/manual/transcoding/) the albums (or a whole playlist with **Misc… › Transcode All**) and leave the results in the output folder. When a transcoded copy exists, Tauon plays it instead of downloading the track.

## Another Tauon on your network

The **Tauon** tile lets you import playlists from another computer running Tauon, and stream music from it.

:::caution[Security]
This connection is not authenticated or encrypted. Only use it on a trusted home network, and never expose the port to the internet.
:::

<Steps>
1. On the computer with the music, go to **Settings › Connect › Sharing** and turn on **Enable remote control**, then restart Tauon.
2. Find that computer's IP address. On Linux, `ip a` shows it.
3. On the other computer, click the **Tauon** tile and enter the IP address (just the address, no `http://` or port).
4. Enter the name of a playlist on the first computer and click **Get playlist**.
</Steps>

This is read-only: you can play the music, but not manage the other computer's collection.
