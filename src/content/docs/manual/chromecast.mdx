## Cast to a device

<Steps>
1. Open the main menu and choose **Chromecast…**, then **Cast to:** your device.
2. Use the playback controls as normal. "Chromecast Mode" appears under the activity indicator at the top right while you're casting.
3. To stop, open **Chromecast…** again and choose **End Cast**.
</Steps>

Tauon converts audio formats that Chromecast can't play, such as high-resolution FLAC or tracks in CUE sheets, so most music casts without any setup. Tracks from network servers like Jellyfin also work, as long as the Chromecast can reach the server.

:::note
Devices are found when Tauon starts. If you turned your Chromecast on after starting Tauon, restart Tauon.
:::

:::tip
If you have a firewall on your computer, allow incoming as well as outgoing connections for Tauon.
:::

Playback speed can't be changed while casting.
