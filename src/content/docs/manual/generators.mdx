A smart playlist fills itself from your other playlists by following a few steps you choose. For example: every synth album from the last few years, shuffled.

:::tip[Recommended workflow]
Import your whole library into one **source playlist**. Then create and delete smart playlists from it as you like.
:::

## Create one

Press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `E`, or right-click a playlist tab and choose **Smart Playlist…**. To start from a ready-made playlist, such as **Unplayed**, **Recently added** or **One-hour mix**, open **Presets**.

<Image src={editorImg} alt="The smart playlist editor" width={682} densities={[1, 2]} />

## Build it from steps

The editor has three rows, and the steps run from left to right:

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **From** | Which playlists to use. Leave it empty to use all of them. |
| **Take** | Which tracks to take: all tracks, or an artist, genre, search, folder and so on. You need at least one. |
| **Then** | What to do with them: filter by year, rating or play count, sort or shuffle, and limit the number of tracks. |

Use **+** on a row to add a step. Click a step to change it, and drag steps to reorder them.

<Image src={stepImg} alt="Editing a Year step" width={299} densities={[1, 2]} />

The playlist updates as you go. The line at the bottom shows how many tracks match, or what needs fixing.

## Keep it up to date

- Tick **Auto update** to regenerate the playlist when you open it and after importing music.
- Without it, the playlist follows **Settings › Library › Playlists › Auto reload playlists**. Shuffled playlists only update automatically when **Auto update** is ticked.
- To update it yourself, right-click its tab and choose **Regenerate** (`Alt` + `R`).

If you rename a playlist that a smart playlist draws from, the smart playlist is updated to match.

To turn a smart playlist back into a normal one, open the editor and choose **Convert to normal playlist**. It keeps its current tracks.

## Other ways to make one

- Right-click a tab and choose **Derive…** to make a smart playlist from that playlist, such as **Top Played Tracks**, **Shuffled Albums** or **Has Lyrics**.
- Click an artist, genre or folder in [global search](/manual/search/), or press `Shift` + `Enter` in quick find.

## Generator codes

Each step is saved as a short code, shown in the **Generator code** box. If you'd rather type codes yourself, see the [generator code reference](/manual/generator-codes/).
