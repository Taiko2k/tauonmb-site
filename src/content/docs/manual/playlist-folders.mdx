Playlist folders let you group playlists in the left panel's playlist list. Folders can contain other folders, up to three levels deep.

<Image src={playlistFoldersImg} alt="Playlist folders in the left panel" width={410} densities={[1, 2]} />

To see your playlists and folders, show the left panel. Right-click the side-panel button (the third button in the top-left corner) and choose **Playlists + Queue**.

## Create a folder

Right-click empty space in the playlist list and choose **New Folder**. Type a name and press `Enter`.

To put playlists in a folder, drag them onto it. You can also drag folders into other folders. When you drag over a closed folder and hold for a moment, it opens.

## Folder options

Right-click a folder for:

- **Rename**
- **Pin to Top Panel**: show the folder as a tab in the top panel. Clicking the tab drops down a list of its playlists.
- **Colour…**: choose the folder's colour
- **New Subfolder** and **New Playlist**: create them inside this folder
- **Delete Folder**: removes the folder but keeps its playlists. They move up to where the folder was.

Click a folder to open or close it.

To take a playlist out of a folder, right-click its tab or entry and choose **Remove from Folder**, or drag it out.

## Pinning

Every playlist can be pinned or unpinned. Pinned playlists have their own tab in the top panel. Unpinned playlists only appear in the left panel. Right-click a tab or a playlist entry and choose **Pin** or **Unpin**.

Pinning a folder instead keeps the top panel tidy: one tab gives you all the playlists inside it.
